# SOP-04: Label Compliance

## Purpose
Keeps consumer-facing claims truthful and auditable.

## Claim definitions
Clean label means no artificial preservatives, colors, or flavors, and
every ingredient must be recognizable to shoppers. Reduced sugar means
at least 25 percent less sugar than the reference product. High protein
means at least 10 grams of protein per serving. Dairy free means no
milk-derived ingredients anywhere in the formula.

## Sign-off
A label claim may only be printed after the label specialist verifies
the formula against the claim definition and records the evidence.
Claim sign-offs are confirmation-gated: a second person approves.

## Changes
Any ingredient change after sign-off voids the claim until it is
re-verified. The audit log records every sign-off and every voiding.
