# SOP-02: Substitution Guidelines

## Purpose
Controls how ingredients are swapped during reformulation so that
function, cost, and label claims stay consistent.

## Candidate rules
A substitute is only a candidate when it targets the same label claim
as the reformulation brief. Candidates with a functional match below
0.50 are rejected outright — they are different ingredients, not
substitutes.

## Scoring
Candidates are ranked by a weighted score: half functional match, three
tenths cost position, two tenths label impact. Cost deltas are always
reported per kilogram and are never netted against other changes.
Label impact is recorded as improves, neutral, or worsens.

## Approval
The top-ranked candidate goes to a pilot trial. If the pilot fails on
texture or sensory, the next candidate is trialled. No substitution
ships without at least one passing pilot.
