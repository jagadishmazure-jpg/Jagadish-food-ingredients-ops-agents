# SOP-01: Formulation Process

## Purpose
Defines the standard stages for developing a new food-ingredient
formulation at Verdant Pantry Foods, from brief to approved formula.

## Stages
A formulation moves through four stages: draft, in-trial, approved,
rejected. A draft becomes in-trial when the first pilot batch is mixed.
Approval requires two consecutive passing trials and a signed label
review. Rejection happens when three trials fail the same criterion or
the micro panel fails twice.

## Roles
The R&D formulator owns the draft. The QA reviewer checks trial records.
The label specialist signs off any consumer-facing claim. No single
person may approve their own formulation.

## Records
Every stage change is written to the formulation log with the actor,
the timestamp, and the reason. Trial batches reference the formulation
version they were mixed from.
